About

Bera Creative Studio is a founder-led technology studio based in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. We build digital products and practical AI solutions for businesses, entrepreneurs and individuals.

The studio was founded by Chidera Emmanuel Ohamadike, a software developer with a focus on artificial intelligence, machine learning and data-driven systems. Our approach is simple: technology should solve problems, simplify processes and create room to grow.
